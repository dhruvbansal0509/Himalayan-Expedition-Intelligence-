# Himalayan Expedition Intelligence

An interactive Power BI dashboard exploring historical Himalayan mountaineering expeditions and their patterns, outcomes, and risks.

## Dashboard pages

- **Himalayan Expedition Intelligence** — overview of expedition activity and key indicators.
- **Peak Explorer** — peak-wise and regional exploration of mountaineering activity.
- **Risk & Expedition Performance** — expedition outcomes, success rates, mortality patterns, and operational risks.
- **Climber & Expedition Profile** — climber demographics, nationality, participation, and team-level analysis.
